## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Weak-to-Strong Generalization via Direct On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05394)

*Shiyuan Feng, Huan-ang Gao, Haohan Chi et al.*

It distills the teacher’s pre/post-RL log-policy shift on student rollouts, beating step-matched RL with 10-point AIME gains in four hours on eight A100s.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/60294a1f-weak-to-strong-generalization-via-direct-on-policy?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Ring-Zero: Scaling Zero RL to a Trillion Parameters for Emergent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12395)

*Xinyu Tang, Gangqiang Cao, Yurou Liu et al.*

Scales verifiable-reward zero-data RL to a 1T-parameter LLM with concise reasoning.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/4c4ac522-ring-zero-scaling-zero-rl-to-a-trillion-parameters-for?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Function-Aware Fill-in-the-Middle as Mid-Training for Coding Agent Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12463)

*Yubo Wang, Jiarong Liang, Yuxuan Zhang et al.*

Uses PDG-selected whole-function FIM mid-training to simulate tool-return context, improving SWE-Bench-Verified by up to 3.2 points across three code models.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/8d7b9458-function-aware-fill-in-the-middle-as-mid-training-for?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [Robbyant/lingbot-video](https://github.com/Robbyant/lingbot-video)

Targets embodied video generation with MoE speedups, unifying T2I/T2V/TI2V and posting 0.620 RBench.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/6ae6e655-lingbot-video?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [mira-wm/mira](https://github.com/mira-wm/mira)

Unlike mostly single-agent or offline world models, it simulates synchronized four-player Rocket League at 20 FPS on one GPU and releases a matching dataset.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/e67e20aa-mira?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [xinyan-cxy/OpenCoF](https://github.com/xinyan-cxy/OpenCoF)

Unlike speed-focused video generators, it adds a 17,312-video supervision set for temporal reasoning.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/612f7aa9-opencof?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [ai-sage/TimeGround-1M](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai-sage/TimeGround-1M)

Unlike typical speech datasets, it pairs 14.2k hours of long audio with three aligned timestamped supervision views up to 120 minutes.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/c592298c-timeground-1m?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [How Fast Do You Forget? 13M Duolingo Sessions](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kylefengkfeng209/how-fast-do-you-forget-13m-duolingo-sessions)

Unlike most datasets, it captures real-world forgetting curves from 12.85M learner sessions across six languages.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/03cb31c5-how-fast-do-you-forget-13m-duolingo-sessions?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [AI Model Arena Rankings (2023-2026)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/riyagarg0314/ai-model-arena-rankings-2023-2026)

Unlike point-in-time leaderboards, it provides 97,377 historical Elo snapshots across five arenas.
