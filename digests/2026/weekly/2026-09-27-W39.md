## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [RecreationWorld: Scalable and Verifiable Environments for Hybrid Computer-Use Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22000)

*Shuai Bai, Jiayong Deng, Yikun Fu et al.*

It uniquely benchmarks hybrid agents by requiring live app inspection, code recreation, and hidden behavior-plus-visual verification across five platforms.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/24fc349a-recreationworld-scalable-and-verifiable-environments-for?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [CodeMidas: Scaling Agentic Coding RL Environments from Code Itself](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22068)

*Bowen Ye, Lei Li, Shicheng Li et al.*

Unlike issue- or test-derived coding RL datasets, it extracts 5,545 executable tasks directly from source code.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/19574a0f-codemidas-scaling-agentic-coding-rl-environments-from-code?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [JEPA-Anything: Learning Predictive Models across Different Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20800)

*Taoyong Cui, Zhongyao Wang, Xinyue Xu et al.*

Unlike monolithic JEPAs, it factorizes latent targets into orthogonal predictors across seven domains.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/cf9ba7df-jepa-anything-learning-predictive-models-across-different?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [jaredpalmer/kev](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev)

Unlike recent typed-decision repos, it packs many question types into one calibrated, decode-free pass with 2.0× speedup and 4e-6 output parity.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/b1b25be5-kev?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [NandhaKishorM/laya](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya)

Unlike similar single-pass classifiers, it routes across English and multilingual checkpoints, covering 45 languages at 7.2 ms/question batched.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/cd5bfbd3-laya?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [mizorewww/laya-mlx](https://github.com/mizorewww/laya-mlx)

Ports typed decision inference to Apple Silicon MLX, matching 63/63 outputs at 7.4-13.4 ms locally.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/5eadc633-laya-mlx?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [secemp9/arxiv-complete](https://huggingface.co/datasets/secemp9/arxiv-complete)

Unlike paper-text corpora, it combines 3.15M arXiv papers with source files, resolved TeX, and 5.03M-version history in one auditable 16.08 TB snapshot.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/d91eb944-arxiv-complete-corpus?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [EgoSteer/EgoSteer-RealWorld](https://huggingface.co/datasets/EgoSteer/EgoSteer-RealWorld)

54,454 real-robot egocentric bimanual episodes across 193 tasks pair RGB-D, 74D actions, and language.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/c2078c99-egosteer-real-world-bimanual-teleoperation-dataset?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [microsoft/ProgramDistill](https://huggingface.co/datasets/microsoft/ProgramDistill)

Unlike GUI-agent benchmarks, it evaluates coding agents by restoring masked web-app features with replayable browser traces and executable verifiers across 4,063 tasks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/c51a6f01-programdistill-bench?utm_source=gh_card)
