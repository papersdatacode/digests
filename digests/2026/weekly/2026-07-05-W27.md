# ML Week 27 of 2026 · Jun 29 – Jul 05

#ML #WeeklyDigest #W27_2026

*From weekly rankings · through Jul 5*

## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Orca: The World is in Your Mind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30534)

*Yihao Wang, Yuheng Ji, Mingyu Cao et al.*

It learns one next-state latent from video and language, then probes frozen backbones for text, images, and robot actions.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/8b3dfd28-orca-the-world-is-in-your-mind?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Improved Large Language Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25331)

*Shen Nie, Qiyang Min, Shaoxuan Xu et al.*

Keeps masked diffusion end-to-end at 8B scale, matching Qwen2.5-7B and surpassing LLaDA by 14.5-21.6 points on reasoning benchmarks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/cca7afad-improved-large-language-diffusion-models?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Scaling the Horizon, Not the Parameters: Reaching Trillion-Parameter Performance with a 35B Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30616)

*Lei Bai, Zongsheng Cao, Yang Chen et al.*

It matches 1T-class agent results with 35B parameters by scaling 45K-token horizons and merging six specialists via domain-routed on-policy distillation.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/be581f96-scaling-the-horizon-not-the-parameters-reaching-trillion?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [deepseek-ai/DeepSpec](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSpec)

Unlike single-method decoding repos, it unifies data prep, multi-GPU training, and evaluation across DSpark, DFlash, Eagle3, and four target-model setups.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/340023c8-deepspec?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Yoonkyo/mu0](https://github.com/Yoonkyo/mu0)

Unlike action-token VLAs, it predicts embodiment-agnostic future 3D interaction traces pretrainable from video alone.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/70cf5b10-mu0?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [xiaobiaodu/Flux-GS](https://github.com/xiaobiaodu/Flux-GS)

Unlike most 3DGS repos, it exports compressed scenes as JSON for mobile/WebGL decoding.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/934863af-flux-gs?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [nvidia/Cosmos3-DROID](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Cosmos3-DROID)

Unlike single-lab teleoperation sets, it spans 71.9K episodes across 564 scenes and 86 tasks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/ad656c45-cosmos3-droid?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Voxel51/SceneFun3D](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Voxel51/SceneFun3D)

Combines 710 Faro-scanned indoor scenes with part-level affordances, motion, language, and 2D/3D correspondences.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/93076fce-scenefun3d?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [MAIR-Lab-HUST/SciIR-82k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MAIR-Lab-HUST/SciIR-82k)

It pairs 82k scientific figures with three-part reasoning annotations and full CC-BY provenance.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/b9835045-sciir-82k?utm_source=gh_card)
