# ML Week 47 of 2026 · Nov 16 – Nov 22

#ML #WeeklyDigest #W47_2026

*From weekly rankings · through Nov 22*

## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [K2-Horizon: A Frontier Fleet of Open Source Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21003)

*K2 Team, Zhengzhong Liu, Haonan Li et al.*

Unlike most open LLM releases, it ships six fully open models up to 375B-A23B with sparse value-routing MoE and draft-free decoding adapters delivering 2.2x speedup.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/279b78e9-k2-horizon-a-frontier-fleet-of-open-source-language-models?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [ForceHarness: Turning Force into Action and Physics for Frozen Frontier Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21099)

*Ke Sun, Kevin Yuchen Ma, Yike Zhang et al.*

It adds a force-control layer beneath frozen robot models, turning 3.3% into 90.0% real-arm success without finetuning.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/3531e9a2-forceharness-turning-force-into-action-and-physics-for?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [π^4: A Foundation Model for 4D Scene Geometry and Motion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23144)

*Yifan Wang, Weiguang Zhao, Ni Yang et al.*

It unifies single-image, multiview, and video 4D geometry-motion prediction in one 0.5B forward pass.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/5cbad37b-4-a-foundation-model-for-4d-scene-geometry-and-motion?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [mit-han-lab/pear](https://github.com/mit-han-lab/pear)

Targets robot control with zero-demonstration, sim-only RL for an open 27B reasoning model, then deploys the same stack to real robots.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/5b431d94-pear?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Vaibhaav-Tiwari/deepseek-v3-jax](https://github.com/Vaibhaav-Tiwari/deepseek-v3-jax)

Distills DeepSeek-V3’s MLA, MoE, MTP, and balancing into 190 pure-JAX lines trainable on a MacBook Air.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/24b8e6e2-deepseek-v3-jax?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [huawei-csl/MACaQ](https://github.com/huawei-csl/MACaQ)

Unlike single-runtime quantizers, it spans text, image, video, and audio with GGUF/W4A4 support, quantizing 122B models in 64 minutes using 32.5GB memory.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/d025f3ea-macaq?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [ropedia-ai/Ropedia-Xperience-2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ropedia-ai/Ropedia-Xperience-2)

Unlike larger egocentric-only datasets, it aligns LiDAR scene scans, mocap, IMU, and task annotations in one shared metric space.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/7904d508-ropedia-xperience-2?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Tokenwave-AI/swe-cowork](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Tokenwave-AI/swe-cowork)

Unlike coding benchmarks with fully specified issues, it tests requirement elicitation via 4-6 simulated teammates holding 2,712 hidden requirements.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/087a4db9-swe-cowork-bench?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [KORMo-VL/Ko-MultimodalMind2Web](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KORMo-VL/Ko-MultimodalMind2Web)

Provides offline Korean web-agent evaluation with 549 human tasks and 12,905 screenshot-action labels.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/5a39294e-ko-multimodalmind2web?utm_source=gh_card)
