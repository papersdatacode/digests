## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [EnvHarness: Awakening Static Worlds for Agent Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19880)

*Chengsong Huang, Zifeng Wang, Rujun Han et al.*

Unlike papers generating new environments, it retrofits existing verifiable ones via rollout-driven modular wrappers.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/0a497bad-envharness-awakening-static-worlds-for-agent-learning?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [On-Policy Self-Distillation in Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24646)

*Wei Zhou, Xiongwei Zhu, Lingdong Kong et al.*

It converts image-level rewards into explicit denoising targets, winning 19/20 settings with up to 44.0% gains and 40-63% less GPU-hours.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/b240a69a-on-policy-self-distillation-in-diffusion-models?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [VBVR-Pro: A Scalable and Verifiable Suite for Native Visual Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26105)

*Junxiang Xu, Ruisi Wang, Fanyi Pu et al.*

It provides 300 procedural visual reasoning tasks with deterministic scorers aligned across image, video, and interleaved generation.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/7d9f9798-vbvr-pro-a-scalable-and-verifiable-suite-for-native-visual?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [ApodexAI/FrontierAgent](https://github.com/ApodexAI/FrontierAgent)

Unlike most agent runtimes, it ships built-in evaluation and shows parallel Agent Team beating ReAct 38.5 vs 34.4 on APEX.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/8f07b7a5-frontieragent?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Leonxlnx/unlazy](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/unlazy)

Unlike orchestration runtimes, it enforces file-based completion gates and stop blocking, reporting 1.6-3.9× more effort and 4-10 self-found defects fixed.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/c314d36e-unlazy?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [zhengzihaoPKU/RoboECC](https://github.com/zhengzihaoPKU/RoboECC)

Targets VLA deployment with offline split search plus online bandwidth-aware cut adjustment, unlike static edge-cloud partitioning.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/9c45c776-roboecc?utm_source=gh_card)
