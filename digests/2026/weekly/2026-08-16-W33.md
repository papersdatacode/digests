## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867)

*Alexander Panfilov, David Schmotz, Ilia Shumailov et al.*

Exploits weaker sibling models as cross-provider decryption oracles, recovering 182 credentials from 315,320 reasoning blobs.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/958b0390-stealing-reasoning-traces-from-proprietary-llm-apis?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Recursive Synthesis for Long-Horizon Terminal Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05466)

*Zhongzhi Li, Yucheng Shi, Zongxia Li et al.*

Recursively grows sandbox-verified terminal tasks from seeds, yielding 37,484 tasks at $0.05 each.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/8432ceea-recursive-synthesis-for-long-horizon-terminal-tasks?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Mechanist: AI as a Scientific Instrument for Discovering the Mechanisms of Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12036)

*Mengru Wang, Junfeng Fang, Shuofei Qiao et al.*

Targets autonomous theory-level mechanistic discovery, grounding experiments in 13k interpretability papers and 32 tools.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/63debc83-mechanist-ai-as-a-scientific-instrument-for-discovering-the?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [jd-opensource/JoyAI-Video-Edit](https://github.com/jd-opensource/JoyAI-Video-Edit)

Unlike offline video editors, it performs causal instruction-guided editing on open-ended streams at 30.19 FPS.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/723980b9-joyai-video-edit?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [yanghaha0908/GROW](https://github.com/yanghaha0908/GROW)

Applies on-policy group-relative reward reweighting directly to flow-matching TTS, improving WER and speaker similarity while training 2.9× faster than DiTAR-GRPO.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/4c343310-grow?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [zju3dv/INTACT-JEPA](https://github.com/zju3dv/INTACT-JEPA)

Unlike search-based world-model controllers, it learns direct goal-conditioned actions, hitting 95.33% success at 2.9-5.5 ms latency.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/cbcc2ba1-intact-jepa?utm_source=gh_card)
