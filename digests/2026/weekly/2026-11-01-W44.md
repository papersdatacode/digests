## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Hy-Nomas: Towards Free-form Interactive World Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36729)

*Qiaoling Chen, Jiaxiang Cheng, Xueqing Deng et al.*

Unlike other world models, it supports multi-turn free-form video interaction by separating persistent scene memory from local action commands.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/713b27dc-hy-nomas-towards-free-form-interactive-world-generation?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [The Trinity:Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36077)

*Weidong Tang, Ruiheng Wang, Jingxuan Zhang et al.*

It clusters tasks by shared capabilities, co-evolving simulators and reusable modules for 34-point gains on 28 benchmarks and 26 points on 18 unseen tasks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/49fbe613-the-trinity-capability-driven-recursive-self-improvement?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Can Discrete Tokens Serve as Semantically Lossless Representations of Visual Signals?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37662)

*Jialv Zou, Xiaotong Li, Zhengrong Yue et al.*

FVQ uses symmetric parallel full-feature codebooks to avoid high-bit saturation, slightly surpassing continuous QwenViT.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/1b5f0379-can-discrete-tokens-serve-as-semantically-lossless?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [CuboiLeo/SuperMimic-Reproduced](https://github.com/CuboiLeo/SuperMimic-Reproduced)

Unlike most control repos, it fully reproduces SuperMimic on Unitree G1, matching 0.82 LAFAN1 in 1.66 hours on one RTX 5090.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/da0e75d2-supermimic-reproduced?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [google-deepmind/detectorscope](https://github.com/google-deepmind/detectorscope)

Unlike black-box AI text detectors, it exposes named feature sums and token-level evidence while preserving accuracy above 0.7 interpretability on COLING and RAID.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/6040cd6f-detectorscope?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [muhammad-talha-ad/LOCUS](https://github.com/muhammad-talha-ad/LOCUS)

Unlike other grounding repos, it exactly recovers greedy coordinate-token boxes in 5.6 passes, running 1.5× faster than vLLM on identical weights.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/a6e37999-locus?utm_source=gh_card)
