## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [NCP-ArchPreview Technical Report: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10715)

*NCP Team, Jiaqi Cao, Chiyu Chen et al.*

Adds product-quantized next-concept prediction to autoregressive decoding, matching OLMo-3-7B loss with 51.3% tokens.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/7cb3060f-ncp-archpreview-technical-report-moving-towards-latent?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [ZGCM-1: A Fully Open and Extremely Efficient Foundation Model for Math and Agentic Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13356)

*Jiyan He, Guang Liang, Hao Liu et al.*

Unlike larger post-trained specialists, it trains an open 7B from scratch to 256K context, ranking first across 14 reasoning benchmarks and reaching 4.2x faster 16K pretraining time-to-loss.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/0846a05f-zgcm-1-a-fully-open-and-extremely-efficient-foundation?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [An Open Recipe for IMO Gold: Training Nemotron for Olympiad Mathematics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10712)

*Ivan Moshkov, Stephen Ge, George Armstrong et al.*

Reached IMO 2026 gold purely with natural-language generate-verify-refine search, without formal tools.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/bd8beabe-an-open-recipe-for-imo-gold-training-nemotron-for-olympiad?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [kevinzakka/mjbatch](https://github.com/kevinzakka/mjbatch)

Unlike GPU-focused simulators, it exposes 4096-way CPU MuJoCo batching with direct NumPy state access.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/ff72dbbc-mjbatch?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [AetherLabsAI/RSIAgent](https://github.com/AetherLabsAI/RSIAgent)

Unlike tool-use agents needing finetuning, it improves OSWorld 2.0 partial credit 71.97→78.98 using frozen-memory exploration across 82 tasks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/b0e20043-rsiagent?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [atria-asi/Atria-Dawn-Preview](https://github.com/atria-asi/Atria-Dawn-Preview)

Unlike agent runtimes, it ships a 744B MoE model with leading tool-use benchmarks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/c8a95fdc-atria-dawn-preview?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [openbmb/UltraData-SFT-Agent-2609](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openbmb/UltraData-SFT-Agent-2609)

Unlike smaller or single-turn tool-use sets, it releases 484k executable multi-turn trajectories with tool schemas, observations, errors, retries, and final answers.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/7c153563-ultradata-sft-agent-2609?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Linzhan/UniML3D](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Linzhan/UniML3D)

Unlike text-to-motion datasets, it canonicalizes 7,430 heterogeneous humanoid, animal, and object skeletons with topology and orientation annotations.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/06da2380-uniml3d?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [CohereLabs/tiny-aya-l2-thinker-multilingual-reasoning](https://huggingface.co/datasets/CohereLabs/tiny-aya-l2-thinker-multilingual-reasoning)

Provides 250k aligned reasoning traces across 44 non-English languages, unlike mostly English-only reasoning datasets.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/4e7f4659-tiny-aya-l2-multilingual-reasoning-44-languages?utm_source=gh_card)
