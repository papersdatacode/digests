## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [S-Space: Exploring Spatial Workspace in Multimodal Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13527)

*Zhiyuan Shi, Xiang Li, Xingzhuo Guo et al.*

It identifies a fixed linear 3D spatial subspace in MLLM hidden states and causally edits it.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/453ffb4a-s-space-exploring-spatial-workspace-in-multimodal-language?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [DiffusionART: Efficient, Stable and Scalable Reinforcement of Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12572)

*Zhenglin Wan, Jiayu Chen, Zhengfeiyang Zhang et al.*

Prompt-aggregated reverse-rollout guidance plus trust-region restoration matches baselines using 12x fewer GPU hours.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/f570c411-diffusionart-efficient-stable-and-scalable-reinforcement-of?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Player-1: Learning to Play from Synthetic Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20584)

*Mingyu Ouyang, Yiran Wang, Siyuan Hu et al.*

Uses synthetic RL across 16 verifiable games to learn transferable computer-use control, boosting MineDojo-300 success from 10.54% to 38.33%.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/f47e18e9-player-1-learning-to-play-from-synthetic-experience?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [NandhaKishorM/vegaml](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/vegaml)

Unlike recent typed-decision repos, it adds local multimodal decisions with conformal sets and abstention.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/83bcea78-vegaml?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [rockmatrix-ai/YANchor](https://github.com/rockmatrix-ai/YANchor)

Unlike most long-context repos, it pairs a recurrent 4B model with O(1) memory and H100 serving that sustains 512 sequences at 12,431 tok/s.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/0fde111c-yanchor?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Optima-CityU/OpenArchEvo](https://github.com/Optima-CityU/OpenArchEvo)

Searches executable PyTorch architecture programs beyond fixed NAS spaces, yielding spiking models with 31.7× lower estimated arithmetic energy.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/e096ceaf-openarchevo?utm_source=gh_card)
