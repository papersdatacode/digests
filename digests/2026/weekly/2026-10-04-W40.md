## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [YuE2: Unifying Symbolic and Audio Music Generation at Frontier Quality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33757)

*Ruibin Yuan, Jiahao Pan, Junyan Jiang et al.*

Unlike end-to-end audio models, it explicitly plans readable scores before full-song rendering.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/6144b80e-yue2-unifying-symbolic-and-audio-music-generation-at?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [TabFM: A Zero-Shot Foundation Model for Tabular Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37959)

*Weihao Kong, Erez Louidor Ilan, Shuxin Nie et al.*

A 400M model trained only on synthetic SCM tables beats tuned AutoGluon zero-shot.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/6e794885-tabfm-a-zero-shot-foundation-model-for-tabular-data?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [CoWindow Attention: Full Causal Coverage Is a Collective Property](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32704)

*Jingze Shi, Zhangyang Peng, Xianduo Li et al.*

Unlike routed sparse attention, it guarantees full causal coverage collectively across heads, preserving 8K recall while cutting 128K train latency 7.4×/8.6× and decode 3.0×.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/561e9c6e-cowindow-attention-full-causal-coverage-is-a-collective?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [PostHog/jeeves](https://github.com/PostHog/jeeves)

Unlike single-pass decision models, it adds reasoning to a Jev-compatible 9B classifier, raising JevBench from 0.866 to 0.935 while keeping calibrated outputs and optional 0.3-second no-think inference.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/a3c98983-jeeves?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [ninjahawk/livenerf](https://github.com/ninjahawk/livenerf)

Unlike static benchmarks, it append-only tracks post-launch model drift daily with pinned prompts and pre-registered statistics.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/aab7eafd-livenerf?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Contrastive-LM/CLM](https://github.com/Contrastive-LM/CLM)

Unlike typed-decision classifiers, it scores state-action pairs for routing, ranking, and verifiers at up to 9× lower latency than Jev.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/697ce950-clm?utm_source=gh_card)
