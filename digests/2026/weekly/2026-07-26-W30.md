## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [TimeLens2: Generalist Video Temporal Grounding with Multimodal LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17423)

*Yuhan Zhu, Changlian Ma, Xiangyu Zeng et al.*

It models temporal grounding as interval sets, using matching-free Wasserstein RL and multi-span labels.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/8ecfee43-timelens2-generalist-video-temporal-grounding-with?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Xiaomi-Robotics-1: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models with over 100K Hours of Real-World Trajectories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15330)

*Xiaomi Robotics Team, Jun Guo, Piaopiao Jin et al.*

Uses 100K+ hours of auto-labeled real trajectories, replacing scarce teleoperated task labels.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/d8872515-xiaomi-robotics-1-scaling-vision-language-action-models?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [RynnBrain 1.1: Towards More Capable and Generalizable Embodied Foundation Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17977)

*Kehan Li, Bohan Hou, Minghao Zhu et al.*

It adds native 3D grounding, contact-point prediction, and masked cross-embodiment actions, then tops three embodied benchmarks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/e2e7161c-rynnbrain-1-1-towards-more-capable-and-generalizable?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [Red-EAD/FlowBlock](https://github.com/Red-EAD/FlowBlock)

Unlike draft-model decoding work, it parallelizes diffusion LM blocks training-free, reaching 2.95× TPS.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/0836829b-flowblock?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-Embodied-RxBrain-1.0](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-Embodied-RxBrain-1.0)

Unlike single-task world models, it unifies VQA, 4-frame prediction, and action-image planning in one released 6.2B model.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/0852c962-hy-embodied-rxbrain-1-0?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [PengZhang64/circuit-framework](https://github.com/PengZhang64/circuit-framework)

Unlike trading-agent repos, it enforces deterministic risk approval and preserves NO\_TRADE outcomes.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/8e8e5965-circuit-framework?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [open-alchemy/code-alchemy](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-alchemy/code-alchemy)

Near-trillion-token scale unifies rewriting, QA, dev tasks, dialogue, and execution traces.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/6f9a1c55-code-alchemy?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [FINAL-Bench/Aether-7B-5Attn-checkpoints](https://huggingface.co/datasets/FINAL-Bench/Aether-7B-5Attn-checkpoints)

Releases three raw 7B pretraining checkpoints with weights, config, and tokenizer for direct trajectory analysis.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/7036ea4e-aether-7b-5attn-intermediate-pretraining-checkpoints?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Expert Label Noise: 176 Annotators, Known Truth](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/georgymamarin/geosteering-world-cup-2021-expert-interpretations)

Unlike most annotation datasets, it provides 176 expert labels on identical cases with known truth, enabling direct measurement of expert noise and aggregation accuracy.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/68e23e2d-expert-label-noise-176-annotators-known-truth?utm_source=gh_card)
