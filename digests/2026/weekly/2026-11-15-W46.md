*From weekly rankings · through Nov 15*

## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [World In-Context Learning for Agentic Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12885)

*Heng Fang, Zehui Guo, Yuehan Zhang et al.*

It learns one-shot manipulation by simulation rehearsal into reusable text memory, reaching 83.4% on 50 RoboTwin tasks without gradient updates.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/8208651b-world-in-context-learning-for-agentic-robot-manipulation?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [FrontierPhysics: Evaluating Agents for End-to-End Physics Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06910)

*Bingran You, Wenjun Ke, Ria Rosenauer et al.*

Uses physicist-authored open-ended tasks with publishability grading, not fixed-answer or reproduction benchmarks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/15b59578-frontierphysics-evaluating-agents-for-end-to-end-physics?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Open-HumanScale: An Open Recipe for Scaling Human and Robot Experience for Embodied Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06817)

*Juncheng Ma, Jiaxin Li, Jiankai Tu et al.*

Uses 50k hours of egocentric human video for joint human-robot pretraining, reaching 88.65% RoboTwin 2.0 zero-shot manipulation.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/4d44f615-open-humanscale-an-open-recipe-for-scaling-human-and-robot?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [Robbyant/lingbot-touch](https://github.com/Robbyant/lingbot-touch)

Unifies magnetic, piezoresistive, and visuotactile inputs into shared tactile tokens for zero-training cross-sensor transfer.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/0301ddc4-lingbot-touch?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [kIieret/facebookresearch-ProgramBench](https://github.com/kIieret/facebookresearch-ProgramBench)

Targets black-box program reconstruction from binaries plus docs, scored by hidden behavioral tests.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/7820dda6-facebookresearch-programbench?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Einsia/Frontier3D](https://github.com/Einsia/Frontier3D)

Benchmarks coding agents on 145 reproducible end-to-end 3D creation tasks across Blender and Unreal.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/706c778f-frontier3d?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [tencent/TailorRig](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tencent/TailorRig)

It releases 39,073 humanoid rigs pairing meshes, skeletons, and sparse skinning weights.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/0f5d1228-tailorrig?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Sandflow/sand3d-articraft](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-articraft)

Unlike mesh-only rig datasets, it releases 9,209 executable Python/OpenUSD articulated objects with 33,444 explicit joints.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/0bcd2257-sand3d-articraft?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [zkniu/VoxCanvas](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zkniu/VoxCanvas)

Unlike recent datasets, it provides 41.4M bilingual speech-editing pairs spanning nonverbal edits with grounded edit spans and paired real/synthetic audio.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/f9d66b16-voxcanvas-41m?utm_source=gh_card)
