## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Kimi K3: Open Frontier Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24653)

*Kimi Team, Tongtong Bai, Yifan Bai et al.*

Unlike most open models, it combines 2.8T MoE pretraining with test-time RL, 1M context, and 76.9 ProgramBench/30.8 AutomationBench results.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/972fee3c-kimi-k3-open-frontier-intelligence?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [TurboVLA: Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Model at 32 Hz on an RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27205)

*Hengyi Xie, Chenfei Yao, Xianjin Wu et al.*

Unlike LLM-centered VLAs, it delivers 32 Hz control at 0.9 GB VRAM.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/7b9b8436-turbovla-real-time-vision-language-action-model-at-32-hz-on?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [HumanCLAW: Can Vision-Language Models Act Through a Body?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27180)

*Siyao Li, Jiawei Gu, Shuai Liu et al.*

It isolates VLM decision quality from humanoid motor failures across 1,218 episodes.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/c4ced412-humanclaw-can-vision-language-models-act-through-a-body?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [slvDev/esp32-ai](https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai)

Runs a 28.9M-parameter LLM on an $8 ESP32-S3 by flash-streaming rows, far beyond prior ~260K-parameter microcontroller models.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/824d10f3-esp32-ai?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [nwneel/Video-ChatGPT](https://github.com/nwneel/Video-ChatGPT)

Unlike recent video repos, it pairs a video-chat model with a 100K instruction dataset and benchmark, while beating prior zero-shot baselines on four QA sets.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/64def581-video-chatgpt?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [sii-research/tau-0-vla](https://github.com/sii-research/tau-0-vla)

Combines world-model-guided test-time subtask search with cross-embodiment control trained on 40,115 real-robot hours.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/16443198-tau-0-vla?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [simple-world-lab/HiFi-UMI-2K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/simple-world-lab/HiFi-UMI-2K)

Unlike mostly synthetic or single-view corpora, it provides 2,000 hours of calibrated six-view bimanual manipulation with 20D trajectories, gripper states, language, and QC metadata.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/9ff2c012-hifi-umi-2k?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [ByteDance/TableVerse](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ByteDance/TableVerse)

Unlike synthetic tabletop sets, it reconstructs 100K real-image scenes with physics and expert trajectories.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/0e34c074-tableverse?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Indian Labour & Corporate Law (JSON) - 950+section](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vinayak9421/corporate-laws-of-india-structured-json)

Unlike generic legal corpora, it structures 950+ Indian statutory sections across 14 Acts for section-level RAG and compliance retrieval.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/d5597dc2-indian-labour-corporate-law-json-950-section?utm_source=gh_card)
