## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Ropedia W0: Scaling Loco-Manipulation World Models across Embodiments with Human Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26855)

*Tianqi Liu, Hanfei Zhao, Qifeng Liang et al.*

It unifies locomotion and manipulation via camera-pose plus skeleton conditioning across 13k human/robot hours.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/5cd7df79-ropedia-w0-scaling-loco-manipulation-world-models-across?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [FDE-Gym: The Last Mile of AI Delivery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22350)

*Dongxu Yang, Hongda Yan, Renxin Wang et al.*

Unlike agent benchmarks with fixed specs, it measures KPI uplift minus stakeholder-contact cost in ambiguous customer environments.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/eba3518c-fde-gym-the-last-mile-of-ai-delivery?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Agent World Model in the Wild: Computer-Use Agent Environment Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26828)

*Zhaoyang Wang, Qianhui Wu, Haorui Wang et al.*

It synthesizes unified GUI/MCP/CLI executable PC worlds with execution-gated long-horizon tasks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/5a96392e-agent-world-model-in-the-wild-computer-use-agent?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [MemTensor/MemT-1.0](https://github.com/MemTensor/MemT-1.0)

Unlike retrieval-style agent memory, it persists latent session state directly, cutting 128K-history storage about 66×.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/6402570a-memt-1-0?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [OpenMOSS/MOSS-Image](https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-Image)

Unlike most open text-to-image releases, it combines JSON layout control, readable text rendering, and 4K-token prompts in a public 5B DiT with fine-tuning support.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/e5cb9fc6-moss-image?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-Memory](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-Memory)

Unlike memory-native models, it unifies chat, task, and code diff-chain memory with zero-LLM-cost writes and retrieval.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/f5e92ec5-hy-memory?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥇 [hongzhuyi/VDEBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hongzhuyi/VDEBench)

Targets dense bilingual document-image editing with region masks and seven text/table edit types.

Releases 1,000 robot hours with synchronized high-resolution tactile, vision, audio, language, and action streams.
