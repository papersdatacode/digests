## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39302)

*Randall Balestriero, Yann LeCun*

It proves cross-modal JEPA encoders align up to rotation and beats 72B LMs with 42x fewer samples.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/071c4c17-proving-and-improving-the-platonic-representation?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Wave Forcing: Towards Speed-of-Light Streaming Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00859)

*Yijie Jin, Yuxuan Liu, Jiajun Xu et al.*

It pipelines denoising with cache refresh to stream one video chunk per forward pass.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/e84bc392-wave-forcing-towards-speed-of-light-streaming-video?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [VibeVideoBench: Evaluating Multimodal Agents on Code-Driven Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35864)

*Muxi Diao, Wenbin Xu, Lele Yang et al.*

It benchmarks autonomous code-written MP4 generation in 1-hour sandboxes with evidence-based grading across 239 tasks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/d2b27865-vibevideobench-evaluating-multimodal-agents-on-code-driven?utm_source=gh_card)
