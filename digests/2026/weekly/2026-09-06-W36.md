## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Repo-To-Skill: Distilling GitHub Repositories Into AI4AI Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02749)

*Jianlyu Chen, Yuyang Hu, Hongjin Qian et al.*

It adds verified, reusable skill graphs distilled from repositories, boosting fixed-budget research agents 134.3% on MLE-bench.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/1781f19e-repo-to-skill-distilling-github-repositories-into-ai4ai?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886)

*Junchao Huang, Guian Fang, Shengju Qian et al.*

Standardizes 10 video sources and trains 5B-33B backbones for hours-long rollouts from 5-second clips.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/43639392-solarwm-open-data-and-scalable-training-for-long-horizon?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Does On-Policy Distillation Really Distill? From Noisy Teacher to Self-Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31046)

*Yi Ding, Ruqi Zhang*

It replaces noisy teacher-guided on-policy distillation with teacher-free entropy-scaled token penalties, gaining +35.41 Avg@32 on AIME24.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/a3a9d3a3-does-on-policy-distillation-really-distill-from-noisy?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [Tencent/WeMM-Embedding](https://github.com/Tencent/WeMM-Embedding)

Unlike single-modality embedders, it unifies five input types in one space and retains 98.7% image/video performance at 256 dimensions.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/278fba27-wemm-embedding?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [MLO-lab/LeVJEPA](https://github.com/MLO-lab/LeVJEPA)

Eliminates EMA targets, predictors, and masking yet beats V-JEPA 2 with 5.6-20.8× less pretraining compute.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/73eafc81-levjepa?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [amap-cvlab/ABot-Recon](https://github.com/amap-cvlab/ABot-Recon)

Streams monocular 3D reconstruction causally over arbitrary-length videos with fixed 12-frame memory.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/a9bfc675-abot-recon?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [microsoft/XL-DocBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/microsoft/XL-DocBench)

Unlike broad corpora, it tests evidence-grounded QA over documents up to 2,935 pages with page-level evidence, cross-document questions, abstention, and only 38.36% top accuracy.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/c51ca469-xl-docbench?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [OpenDataArena/Spark-234K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenDataArena/Spark-234K)

Unlike raw-text scientific corpora, it builds 234K QA pairs from paper-level reasoning skeletons with claims, evidence, quantities, assumptions, and limits.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/6035537a-spark-234k?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [DISLab/Q-CARE](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DISLab/Q-CARE)

Unlike larger QA datasets, it pairs 800 queries with 16 RAG outputs each for reference-free evaluation across both retrieval and generation.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/f2545621-q-care-benchmark?utm_source=gh_card)
