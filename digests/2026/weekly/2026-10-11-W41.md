*From weekly rankings · through Oct 11*

## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Long-WAM: Scaling the Context of World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10528)

*Wei Huang, Bohan Zhang, Chenzhi Liu et al.*

Unlike bidirectionally adapted WAMs, it preserves causal video pretraining, enabling 19.2-second histories to improve control.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/9a3eb907-long-wam-scaling-the-context-of-world-action-models?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [OneStreamer: Unifying Perception, Memory, and Proactive Response in Streaming Video Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01762)

*Xiangyu Zeng, Yuandong Yang, Zhiqiu Zhang et al.*

It unifies proactive response timing and reusable caption memory in one causal decoder.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/ecc200aa-onestreamer-unifying-perception-memory-and-proactive?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [ALoDLM: Adaptively Looped Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04198)

*Liancheng Fang, Zhuowei Li, Youngeun Kim et al.*

It allocates denoising compute per token, letting an 8B DLM beat AR baselines while decoding 2.7× faster on GSM8K.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/196ec779-alodlm-adaptively-looped-diffusion-language-models?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [nokia-applied-research/AnyJev](https://github.com/nokia-applied-research/AnyJev)

Unlike recent typed-decision repos, it retrofits open LLMs into calibrated classifiers from one prefill, boosting BANKING77 auto-decidable coverage 7.7%→52.0% at ≤5% error.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/554d4153-anyjev?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [PSRben/VisionHOPE](https://github.com/PSRben/VisionHOPE)

Unlike most vision backbones, it uses self-modifying memory updates and ships checkpoints across classification, detection, and segmentation.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/23b722e7-visionhope?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Tier-Flow/LoopVL](https://github.com/Tier-Flow/LoopVL)

Unlike most model repos, it releases reproducible recurrent VLM evaluation and visual-state analyses across 16 benchmarks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/ea09a363-loopvl?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [ScholarCatalyst/ScholarCatalyst](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScholarCatalyst/ScholarCatalyst)

Unlike topical paper corpora, it benchmarks inspiration retrieval with 894 author-verified positives, hard negatives, and rationales.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/556a1d30-scholarcatalyst?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [allenai/fetchman-data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/fetchman-data)

352,696 humanoid pickup episodes jointly align language, proprioception, actions, and head+wrist egocentric video.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/e6c93eaa-fetchman-data?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [RSNA Knee 2.5D ConvNeXt reader](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/goodpjw2008/rsna-knee-2-5d-convnext-reader)

Releases end-to-end DICOM inference code and weights for a 0.929 public-LB knee MRI baseline.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/696efe8f-rsna-knee-2-5d-convnext-reader?utm_source=gh_card)
