*From weekly rankings · through Sep 13*

## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [DriveZero: End-to-End Driving Beyond Human Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06055)

*Hao He, Chengcheng Hu, Zirun Su et al.*

Unlike imitation-heavy driving papers, it reaches 95.3 PDMS camera-only without human trajectory supervision.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/2baf37cd-drivezero-end-to-end-driving-beyond-human-demonstrations?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Scaling Automatic Research Agents via World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12564)

*Xiyuan Yang, Sheikh Sarwar, Jingru Cheng et al.*

Uses learned world-model rewards with anchor-based bias correction, cutting RL training 3-4×.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/e32d2d08-scaling-automatic-research-agents-via-world-models?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [LLaDA-Image: Building Strong Image Generators with Fully Open Training Recipes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03796)

*Chuyan Chen, Haoxing Chen, Kun Chen et al.*

Uses mostly real image-only pretraining, then frozen dLLM conditioning and 2-4-step distillation.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/efeb84f3-llada-image-building-strong-image-generators-with-fully?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [OpenWAM-Official/OpenWAM](https://github.com/OpenWAM-Official/OpenWAM)

Unlike single-purpose world-model repos, it releases an open modular WAM stack and pretrained checkpoints trained on 518.5M frames.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/be6f89e4-openwam?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Instituto-IA-LNCC/manaca-1b-base](https://github.com/Instituto-IA-LNCC/manaca-1b-base)

Unlike most LLM releases, it is fully reproducible end-to-end on 2×24GB GPUs.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/89c9004d-manaca-1b-base?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [ifm-ai/uno](https://github.com/ifm-ai/uno)

Unlike speculative decoding repos, it parallelizes exact multi-token sampling without a draft model.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/1b9e1d1c-uno?utm_source=gh_card)

## 📊 Top 3 Datasets

### 🥇 [openbmb/UltraData-Code](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openbmb/UltraData-Code)

Unlike typical code corpora, it pairs 400B scored algorithmic code tokens with 150B implementation-grounded exercise records across 11 languages.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/f4b813a9-ultradata-code?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [spatialverse/UniPhys-Bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/spatialverse/UniPhys-Bench)

Unlike typical 3D asset sets, it unifies human-verified kinematics, part physics, and object mass/scale on 1,927 simulation-ready articulated objects.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/91d7a99b-uniphys-bench?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [tencent/OSWorkerBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tencent/OSWorkerBench)

Unlike prior GUI datasets, it pairs 100 executable office workflows with step-level demonstrations.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/datasets/55f97844-osworkerbench?utm_source=gh_card)
