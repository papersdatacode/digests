# ML Week 34 of 2026 · Aug 17 – Aug 23

*From weekly rankings · through Aug 23*

## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [SPADE: Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19197)

*Bo Liu, Simon Yu, Yiding Jiang et al.*

It jointly learns to generate executable Gym environments and solve them, outperforming fixed-environment self-play by +5.3 average across eight held-out benchmarks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/e1c824c4-spade-self-play-in-adaptive-synthetic-executable?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Self-Supervised Visual On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14144)

*Yijiang Li, Yijun Liang, Yunjie Tian et al.*

Unlike teacher- or label-dependent distillation, it uses clean-versus-degraded views to self-distill, lifting Qwen3.5-4B from 70.7% to 77.4% and surpassing Qwen3-VL-235B.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/24c984a6-self-supervised-visual-on-policy-distillation?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [OpenART: Scaling Agent Red Teaming via Open-Ended Environment Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00677)

*Yunhao Chen, Xin Wang, Yixu Wang et al.*

Targets stateful agents by evolving shared environment state, reaching 85.0% ASR across 75 setups.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/1e34ce4a-openart-scaling-agent-red-teaming-via-open-ended?utm_source=gh_card)

## 💻 Top 3 Repos

### 🥇 [MirroS-Lab/HarnessEval-W](https://github.com/MirroS-Lab/HarnessEval-W)

Unlike scalar-only video benchmarks, it uses model-agnostic skill routing with auditable evidence trees.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/f0ed4afa-harnesseval-w?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [TencentARC/SCoPE](https://github.com/TencentARC/SCoPE)

Adds explicit camera-ray token coordinates to a pretrained video DiT with under 0.1% new parameters.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/2de709f2-scope?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [AlayaLab/Evoke](https://github.com/AlayaLab/Evoke)

Unlike recent camera-control video models, it sustains long interactive rollouts with bounded memory and mid-rollout re-prompting in three CFG-free steps.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/repos/edcf8ead-evoke?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥇 [Anthropic/claude-protein-binder-design](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/claude-protein-binder-design)

It links 1,440 de novo binders to raw two-vendor kinetics, structures, and stepwise design provenance.
