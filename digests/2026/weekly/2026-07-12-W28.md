## 📄 Top 3 Papers

### 🥇 [Program-as-Weights: A Programming Paradigm for Fuzzy Functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02512)

*Wentao Zhang, Liliana Hotsko, Woojeong Kim et al.*

Compiles natural-language fuzzy specs into reusable LoRA programs, beating prompted 32B models with 50x less inference memory.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/d7588b46-program-as-weights-a-programming-paradigm-for-fuzzy?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥈 [Hierarchical Sparse Attention Done Right: Toward Infinite Context Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02980)

*Xiang Hu, Xinyu Wei, Hao Gu et al.*

It learns chunk retrieval inside forward attention, preserving accuracy while extrapolating 64x longer contexts.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/89be2110-hierarchical-sparse-attention-done-right-toward-infinite?utm_source=gh_card)

### 🥉 [Accurate, Interdisciplinary and Transparent Structure-property Understanding with Deep Native Structural Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07708)

*Chen Tang, Yizhou Wang, Jianyu Wu et al.*

Unlike textized or task-specific models, it natively reasons across proteins, molecules, and crystals with inspectable structure-linked traces, reaching SOTA on 67 of 86 benchmarks.

[→ Full breakdown](https://papersdatacode.com/papers/d7b04acb-accurate-interdisciplinary-and-transparent-structure?utm_source=gh_card)
